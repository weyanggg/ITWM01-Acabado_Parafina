Company Overview

Dentistry centered around you.

LUMIÈRE Dental Clinic is a patient-focused dental practice dedicated to providing quality oral healthcare in a comfortable and welcoming environment.

Our clinic combines modern dental techniques, professional expertise, and personalized attention to help every patient achieve a healthier and more confident smile.

From preventive care and routine checkups to cosmetic and restorative treatments, we are committed to making every dental experience comfortable and positive.

Meet Our Founder

Dr. Amelia Laurent

Founder & Lead Dentist

Dr. Amelia Laurent founded LUMIÈRE Dental Clinic with a simple goal: to create a dental practice where patients receive excellent treatment while feeling genuinely cared for.

With years of experience in general and cosmetic dentistry, Dr. Laurent believes that communication and trust are essential parts of quality dental care.

Her leadership continues to shape LUMIÈRE's commitment to professional excellence, patient comfort, and lifelong oral health.
